Defaults come from WooCommerce email settings.

Site sending limits

Shared across campaigns. Fixed UTC windows; 0 means unlimited.

Limits count starts of sending, not verified handoffs. Waiting does not consume retry attempts. Other WooCommerce emails and test emails are unaffected.

Retention

0 = off (default). Cleanup runs hourly after the retention period.

Active, paused, pending and retryable work is excluded. Drafts and saved criteria have a separate lifecycle.

What is removed and retained?

Cleanup removes campaign content and personal recipient/log data. Campaign IDs, email hashes, claims, minimal outcomes, counters and suppression remain for duplicate protection and opt-outs. External server, provider and backup records are outside this cleanup.

Personal data

Use WordPress Tools → Export / Erase Personal Data for account and guest email addresses.

Erasure retains protection hashes and blocks future sending. A claimed worker or active shared content may require erasure to be run again after completion.
